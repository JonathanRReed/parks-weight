How to read it

Both shapes share one scale, north-up. A speck is a speck. Checkmarks and saved places live only on this phone or computer. National park acreage and visitors are 2023 NPS figures. Forest and state-park outlines come from USFS administrative boundaries and USGS PAD-US. Tiny islands are dropped so phones don’t melt. Not an official NPS or Forest Service site.
