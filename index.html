How to read it

Both shapes are projected locally, north-up, then centered on the same point. Area is real. A park that looks like a speck is a speck. The first U.S. national park, Yellowstone, is a useful yardstick: most of the famous Utah parks fit inside it many times over. The Alaska parks are another story — Wrangell–St. Elias is larger than several states.

Acreage and annual visitors are 2023 National Park Service figures. Boundaries are NPS legislative maps. Photos are from Wikimedia Commons. This is an independent project, not an official NPS site.
